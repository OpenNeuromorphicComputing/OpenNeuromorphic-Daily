### [Noise-Adaptive Analog-Digital Preconditioning](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17251v1)
**2026-11-12** | *Shikhar Shah, Rui Peng Li, Vassilis Kalantzis et al.*

> Analog in-memory computing offers the potential to accelerate approximate inverse preconditioning, but hardware errors can degrade preconditioners designed in digital arithmetic. The correction space is constructed by probing the same programmed analog arrays used in the solve, targeting errors from both the coupling omitted by block Jacobi and persistent hardware perturbations. ### [Tailored magnetic domain-wall ratchet effects through density gradients in triangular-hole arrays](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16735v1)
**2026-11-12** | *Julia Herrero-Albillos, María Ángeles Laguna-Marco, María Vélez et al.*

### [The Weight Is in the Reading: Hidden States in Memristors, Memlumors, and Electrochemical Transistors](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12706v1)
**2026-11-09** | *Gonzalo Rivera-Sierra, Heyi Zhang, Daiki Nishioka et al.*

> Physical synapses are routinely described by a single number, the weight, that is identified with a conductance, a current, or a luminescence yield. This identification conflates two different objects: the internal state that the device stores, and the signal obtained when that state is read. Building on Chua's formulation of memristive systems, we separate the state variable, which evolves with the excitation history, from the observation map that converts it into a measured signal, and we define the weight as the outcome of a specified read protocol. ### [Recurrent Modular Structure in Spiking Neural Network Reinforcement Learning](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14262v1)
**2026-11-10** | *Vikram Ramavarapu, Himanshu Udupi, Ashish Gautam*

> Biological neural circuits are both modular and recurrent, with dense communities sparsely linked to one another. ### [Directed Domain Wall Motion by Radio-Frequency Currents](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15305v1)
