### [Tri-Stack-PIM: A Heterogeneous Multi-Die 3D-Integrated Processing-in-Memory Architecture for Efficient LLM Inference](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13644v1)
**2026-11-10** | *Shuao Jia, Zichao Ling, Chen Bai et al.*

> Large language model (LLM) inference during autoregressive decoding is fundamentally memory-bandwidth-bound. Existing three-dimensional (3D) stacked processing-in-memory (PIM) architectures alleviate the off-chip bandwidth wall, yet remain constrained by several limitations: physical layer (PHY), controllers, and direct memory access (DMA) engines compete with arithmetic units for advanced-node base-die area; hybrid-bonding keep-out zones fragment the base-die floorplan and impede physical design; compute tiles built in a dynamic random access memory (DRAM) process are restricted to low logic density, capping achievable throughput; and nonlinear operations offloaded to the host reintroduce a communication bottleneck. Evaluated across multi-head, grouped-query, and multi-query attention (MHA, GQA, MQA) mechanisms with batch sizes 1-32, Tri-Stack-PIM achieves geometric-mean speedups of 4.95x, 2.57x, and 1.68x across all three attention mechanisms and batch sizes over a graphics processing unit (GPU) baseline, AttAcc, and H2-LLM, respectively.

### [The Weight Is in the Reading: Hidden States in Memristors, Memlumors, and Electrochemical Transistors](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12706v1)
**2026-11-09** | *Gonzalo Rivera-Sierra, Heyi Zhang, Daiki Nishioka et al.*

> Physical synapses are routinely described by a single number, the weight, that is identified with a conductance, a current, or a luminescence yield. This identification conflates two different objects: the internal state that the device stores, and the signal obtained when that state is read. Building on Chua's formulation of memristive systems, we separate the state variable, which evolves with the excitation history, from the observation map that converts it into a measured signal, and we define the weight as the outcome of a specified read protocol. Three platforms show the consequences: a memristor, in which the same voltage writes and reads; a memlumor, in which light both modifies the state and produces the photoluminescence that reports it; and an organic electrochemical transistor (OECT), in which the gate writes and the channel reads. In the transistor, two states prepared by different histories give the same channel current at a specific drain bias and become distinguishable again when the bias is changed, while the stored charge persists and the gate current continues to separate them. Published experiments provide precedents: a drain-bias-dependent cancellation of the OECT transient, and trapped charges in perovskite memlumors that are invisible to conventional time-resolved photoluminescence but revealed by a read-write-read sequence. A synaptic weight is therefore not a property of the material alone but of the material together with its readout, and reports of synaptic behavior should specify what is stored, what is measured, and how one is inferred from the other.

### [Recurrent Modular Structure in Spiking Neural Network Reinforcement Learning](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14262v1)
**2026-11-10** | *Vikram Ramavarapu, Himanshu Udupi, Ashish Gautam*

> Biological neural circuits are both modular and recurrent, with dense communities sparsely linked to one another. Spiking neural networks (SNNs) for reinforcement learning are usually feed-forward, and it is unclear how to obtain functional modules in a recurrent SNN or what they buy. A controller community receives the stimulus, each action has its own community, and latent communities carry internal state. How strongly to enforce modularity depends on the task: full modularity helps most on Acrobot, while on LunarLander the dynamic, partial and full regimes perform alike. Overall, these recurrent modular networks can match or even improve upon feed-forward approaches, with improvements in bio-plausibility and energy efficiency.
